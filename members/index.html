Race against the Use More Kerb community

A championship for members of the channel. Real races, real points, and bragging rights on the channel.

Who can race

Members of Team Use More Kerb and above

Every Team Use More Kerb member (the £2.99 tier and above) can race. You sign up for each race in the #race-entry channel on the Use More Kerb Discord.

How it works

  1. Join Team Use More KerbA YouTube channel membership at £2.99 a month or higher.
  2. Sign up in DiscordTap Sign Up on the race in #race-entry, then Enter Driver Details with your racing name and weight in race kit. There are 30 places on the grid; after that you go on the waiting list and move up if someone drops out. The grid and waiting list show here in the app.
  3. Pay for your raceCheck with the championship organisers on Discord for payment details.
  4. Turn up and racePractice, qualifying and the final.
  5. Score points, top the tableStandings update here after every round.
